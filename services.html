Our Services

A full range of paving, fencing and landscaping services for homes across Manchester.

Block Paving

We lay block-paved driveways, paths and patios using a wide range of block styles and colours. All work includes full ground preparation, sub-base, edging and compaction for a long-lasting finish.

Patios and Slabs

Whether you want natural stone, porcelain or standard concrete slabs, we can design and lay a patio area to suit your garden and budget. We handle everything from excavation through to pointing and finishing.

Fencing

We supply and fit all types of garden and boundary fencing, including close-board, panel, picket and post-and-rail fencing. Old fence removal and disposal is included as standard.

Gravelling

Gravel driveways and paths are a practical, cost-effective option. We lay gravel over a proper membrane and edging to keep everything neat and reduce weed growth.

Brickwork

From garden walls and raised planters to pillar repairs and boundary walls, we carry out all types of exterior brickwork. Matching existing brickwork where needed.

Drainage

We install channel drains, soakaways and surface water drainage as part of driveway and patio projects, or as standalone work. Proper drainage prevents water pooling and protects your property.

Turfing

New lawns laid using quality cultivated turf. We prepare the ground properly, level as needed, and lay turf for a neat, even finish. Advice on aftercare is always provided.

General Landscaping

Full garden landscaping including clearance, levelling, planting areas, raised beds, decorative stone and more. We can work to your own design or help plan a layout that suits your space.
